Support

Help with Holdmie. If your question isn’t answered here, email muneebrafishakoor@gmail.com and a person will reply.

How do I contact you?

Email muneebrafishakoor@gmail.com, or open Holdmie and go to More › Contact us. To report something your partner sent, press and hold it and choose Report. Your User ID helps us find your account quickly: it’s under Account on the plan screen, and in your profile in More as Support ID.

How do we pair?

One of you opens the pairing screen and shares their code. The other types it in. The person who shared the code gets one tap to confirm, or “Not me” if they don’t recognise the request. A code lasts 72 hours, and you can make a new one at any time.

The code doesn’t work

Check each letter with your partner and make sure the code is less than 72 hours old. After 5 wrong tries, wait 10 minutes before trying again. If either of you is still paired with someone else, that person needs to unpair first.

Do we both need to pay?

No. One Holdmie Pro plan covers you both while you’re paired. Your partner sees “Included with [your name]’s plan” and never a price. If the plan ends or you unpair, their access ends too. The terms have the details.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Holdmie › Cancel Subscription, or in Holdmie open More › Subscription › Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. You keep Holdmie Pro until the end of the period you’ve paid for. Deleting the app or your account doesn’t cancel it, and only the person who pays can cancel.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles payments and refunds, and we can’t issue them ourselves. You can ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I have a new phone. How do I get everything back?

Install Holdmie and sign in with the same Apple or Google account as before. Your pairing, everything you shared and your gems come back. If your plan doesn’t show, tap Restore purchases on the plan screen or in More › Subscription, signed in to the Apple Account you paid with. A guest account can’t be moved to a new phone.

We both paid by mistake

Holdmie keeps working, and the app shows the person who bought later a link to manage their plan. One of you can cancel in iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions. We can’t cancel or refund a plan for you, but Apple can consider a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Why do I see the plan screen again?

The plan that covered you ended (it was cancelled and the paid period ran out, or a payment failed), or you unpaired from the person who paid. Nothing you made is deleted. Either of you can choose a plan, or the payer can tap Restore purchases.

Where did the one-time offer go?

It’s shown once per person, the first time you close the plan screen with the small X, and it’s kept with your account. Once you’ve seen it, it doesn’t come back, even after reinstalling.

My gems didn’t arrive

Packs are 500 gems for $0.99 and 3,000 gems for $4.99 (US prices). Gems are added once our server confirms the purchase with Apple, which can take a moment, so open Holdmie again while you’re online. If they’re still missing, email us your User ID and the date of the purchase. Gems belong to your account, so they show on any phone you sign in to.

How does the streak work?

A day counts once both of you have done something in Holdmie on your own date, such as answering a question, sending a drawing, playing a move or ticking a to-do. If you miss a day, either of you can repair it with gems for 3 days afterwards. If Holdmie wasn’t working that day, the repair is free.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Holdmie. Choose a widget and a size, then tap Add Widget. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, choose Lock Screen and tap the widget area.

Why hasn’t my widget changed?

iOS decides when a widget may refresh and limits how often, so a new drawing can take a while to appear, especially in Low Power Mode or when the phone hasn’t been used for some time. Opening Holdmie refreshes every widget, and the small refresh button on the Drawing Board and Partner Status widgets asks again. The sender sees “Delivered” once your phone has received it.

How do I keep private pictures off my Home Screen?

Turn off “Show on my Home Screen” on the Widgets page in Holdmie. Your widgets then show a plain “Open Holdmie” and your notifications show no pictures. If you’ve turned on Sensitive Content Warning in iOS, pictures your partner sends are blurred in the app and kept off your widgets and notifications automatically.

Which notifications will I get?

You choose by type in More › Notifications, where you can also hide the words of messages and turn on a daily reminder (off until you do). A few always come, because they matter for your account or your safety: pairing requests and their answers, being unpaired or your partner deleting their account, your partner starting to share location, and the reminder every 30 days that you’re sharing yours. Holdmie never sends sales notifications.

How does location sharing work?

It’s off until you both turn it on in Us › Location, and Holdmie never turns it on during setup. While Using is enough: your position updates when you open Holdmie, and every 30 seconds while you have the map open. “Update when closed” is an optional extra that lets iOS wake Holdmie after you’ve moved. Each of you chooses what to show: position, battery and network type, plus Wi-Fi name and a 7-day history, which are off by default. You both see the same things about each other. The privacy policy has the details.

How do I stop sharing my location?

Tap Stop sharing on the map, or turn sharing off in Location settings. It stops at once, with no confirmation, and your position, history and status are deleted from our servers. Your partner isn’t notified; they see “Not sharing right now”. Unpairing and deleting your account also stop it.

Why does it say “Location isn’t updating”?

Your partner’s phone isn’t sending readings, usually because Holdmie’s location access was changed in iPhone Settings or by Safety Check. Readings can also be late while a phone is off, offline or in Low Power Mode. “Updated 7m ago” shows how old the last reading is.

What happens if we unpair?

Location and status stop and are deleted for both of you at once. You each keep a copy of the lists, bucket list photos and countdowns, including what the other person added. Drawings, photos, answers and messages are hidden from both of you for 30 days; pair with each other again within that time and everything comes back. After that they’re deleted, and either of you can delete them sooner. See the privacy policy.

How do I block or report someone?

When you unpair, report someone or tap “Not me” on a pairing request, you can block that person; their code is refused from then on, and you can unblock them in More › Blocked. To report something your partner sent, press and hold it and choose Report. To report your partner, use the Report row with their name in More. A person reads every report and replies by email. If something isn’t working, use Report a bug in More.

How do I delete my account?

Go to More › Delete account (it’s also under Account on the plan screen). It happens right away and can’t be undone. Cancel your subscription first in iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions, because deleting your account doesn’t stop Apple’s billing. Your partner gets a short, neutral note, and what you sent becomes “deleted” placeholders on their side. To-dos, bucket list items (with their photos) and countdowns you added stay in their lists without your name.

Which languages does the app speak?

English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Japanese and Korean. You can change it in More › Language. If you and your partner use different languages, each of you reads questions in your own. The privacy policy, the terms and this page are in English.

Do you show ads or sell my data?

No. Holdmie has no ads and no tracking or analytics tools, and we never sell your information. See the privacy policy.

If you feel unsafe

Stop sharing your location with one tap, unpair, and block. iPhone’s Safety Check (Settings › Privacy & Security › Safety Check) can review and stop what you share with anyone. If you’re in danger, call your local emergency number: Holdmie isn’t an emergency service.
