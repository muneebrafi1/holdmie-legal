Who can use the app

You must be 16 or older to use Holdmie. Where the law requires it, the app checks your age range with Apple first, on iOS 26.2 or later. Use Holdmie only with a partner who is 16 or older and who wants to use it with you.

An account is for one person. Keep your phone and your Apple or Google account secure: anyone who can open Holdmie on your phone can see what your partner shares with you, and widgets and notifications can show pictures on your Lock Screen and Home Screen.

Your account

You can start as a guest, then sign in with Apple or Google, which keeps your account, pairing and gems on any iPhone you sign in to. A guest account can’t be recovered on another phone, and guest accounts left unused and unpaired are deleted after 30 days. You’re responsible for what happens in your account.

Pairing

Holdmie is made for two people, and you pair with one partner at a time. One of you shares a code (it lasts 72 hours), the other types it in, and the person who shared the code confirms. Pair only with someone who has agreed to pair with you, on their own phone. Either of you can unpair at any time, alone. The privacy policy explains what happens to what you shared.

Holdmie Pro

Holdmie needs Holdmie Pro. After you sign in, Holdmie shows its plans, and the app opens once you or your partner has an active plan. There’s no free version, but your account always stays in your hands: Restore purchases, your User ID, Terms, Privacy, Sign out and Delete account can always be reached from the plan screen.

Holdmie Pro gives you and your partner the whole app: drawings and photos to each other’s widgets, daily questions and decks, every game, lists, countdowns, location and status sharing, and all the widgets. We may add, change or remove features over time.

If neither of you has an active plan any more, the plan screen comes back for both of you. Nothing you made is deleted, widgets show a plain state, and location sharing pauses until a plan is back.

Plans, prices and the free trial

Holdmie Pro is an auto-renewing subscription. These are the US prices; the App Store shows the price in your currency before you buy, and prices differ by country, with lower prices in some. The price in Apple’s purchase sheet is the price you pay.

Yearly: $49.99 a year. If Apple says you’re eligible, it starts with a 7-day free trial, and the app mentions a trial only then. Weekly: $9.99 a week, charged when you buy, with no trial. One-time offer: the yearly plan at $29.99 a year, described below.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase or, with a free trial, when the trial ends. A subscription renews automatically for the same length and price unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and Apple charges the renewal within the 24 hours before the period ends. If you cancel a free trial at least 24 hours before it ends, you aren’t charged. Any unused part of a free trial ends when you buy another plan. If a price goes up, Apple tells you first and asks for your agreement where the law requires it.

If a renewal payment fails, Apple keeps trying for a while, and Holdmie Pro may stay on during that time.

One plan covers both partners

A plan bought by either of you covers you both while you’re paired. Your partner uses Holdmie Pro on their own account and phone, sees “Included with [your name]’s plan”, and never sees prices or a buy button. Family Sharing isn’t available for Holdmie Pro.

Your partner’s access depends on your plan. It ends when your plan ends (after you cancel and the paid period runs out, when a payment finally fails, or when Apple refunds it), when either of you unpairs, or when you delete your account. Your own access lasts as long as your plan.

If you pair with someone new, your plan covers them once 30 days have passed since it last started covering someone; until then it covers only you. If you restore your plan while signed in to a different Holdmie account, it moves to that account, and the partner it covered loses access.

If you both end up paying, Holdmie works the same, and the app shows the later buyer how to manage their plan. We can’t cancel or refund either plan; each of you manages your own with Apple.

The one-time offer

If you close the plan screen with the small X, Holdmie shows a one-time offer: the yearly plan at $29.99 a year, with no free trial. It’s charged when you buy and renews at $29.99 a year until you cancel. Each person sees it once. It’s kept with your account, so reinstalling the app doesn’t bring it back. Closing it returns you to the plans. There’s no timer.

Cancelling and refunds

Cancelling stops the next renewal, and you keep Holdmie Pro until the end of the period you’ve paid for. Deleting the app or your Holdmie account doesn’t cancel a subscription. Only the person who pays can cancel their plan.

Apple handles all payments and refunds, including any right to cancel a purchase that consumer law gives you where you live. We can’t issue refunds ourselves. You can ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Gems

Gems are a virtual currency inside Holdmie. Each of you earns 10 gems for a finished game, up to 50 a day, and you can buy packs through Apple: 500 gems for $0.99 and 3,000 gems for $4.99 (US prices). You spend gems on hints in games (100 gems) and on repairing a missed streak day (500 gems a day). The app shows what something costs before you spend, and these amounts can change.

Gem packs are consumable purchases. Gems give you a limited licence to use features in Holdmie: they have no cash value, can’t be exchanged for money or moved to another account, and don’t expire while your account exists. They belong to your account, not your phone, and they’re lost if you delete your account. Gem purchases aren’t refundable except where Apple grants a refund; if Apple refunds a pack, we remove those gems, which can leave your balance below zero.

Your streak

Your streak counts the days you both did something in Holdmie, each on your own local date. A day counts once both of you have acted. If you miss a day, either of you can repair it with gems for 3 days afterwards, and the repair is free when the day was missed because Holdmie itself wasn’t working.

Invites

You can invite another couple with your invite link. It works for Holdmie accounts up to 7 days old. Once the invited person pairs, they can claim a free month through Apple if they’re new to Holdmie Pro. It’s a real subscription: after the free month it renews on the plan and at the price shown in Apple’s sheet before they confirm, unless they cancel at least 24 hours before the month ends.

You earn a reward once the invited couple has been paired for 7 days, both of them have signed in with Apple or Google, and one of them has a Holdmie Pro plan through the App Store (their free month counts). If you’re paying for Holdmie Pro and not in a free trial, the reward is a 30-day extension of your subscription through Apple, which allows two a year; otherwise it’s 30 days of Holdmie Pro, which covers your partner too and starts when any plan you already have ends. The first 5 couples a month who pair with your link count, and each person can be invited once. Rewards have no cash value. We may change or end invites and may withhold rewards gained through fake accounts or other abuse; rewards you’ve already received stay.
